it is an illustration, not scientific data.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one clean, minimal logo for BarcodeCNV, a scientific tool connecting lineage barcodes to copy-number alterations. Icon only, no text or letters. Two compact supermarket-style barcodes stacked vertically on the left, each made of a handful of crisp dark vertical bars of varied widths. Immediately beside them on the right, a miniature CNA heatmap: two corresponding rows of long genomic blocks, mostly neutral pale grey with a few contiguous orange gains and blue losses, including a shared event and a distinct event. Unify barcode and heatmap into a single balanced, compact horizontal mark. Flat geometric vector-like design with sharp edges, restrained scientific appearance, easily legible when small. No axes, numbers, arrows, gradients, shadows, chromosome cartoons, DNA helices or mockups. White background, generous but not excessive margin. High resolution.

white canvas with narrow side margins and blank space above and below. It was
